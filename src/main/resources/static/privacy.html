Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for CivicPulse Municipal Redressal Platform

1. Information We Collect

CivicPulse collects and processes the following information to facilitate civic grievance resolution:

  • Citizen Account Data: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and encrypted password hash.
  • Grievance Information: Grievance title, detailed description, municipal category, GPS coordinates (latitude & longitude), and physical street address.
  • Photographic Evidence: Uploaded image attachments (stored securely in a restricted server storage location outside the public web root).
  • Audit & Security Logs: IP address, browser User-Agent, user action type, and timestamps to prevent abuse.

2. Anonymous Filing Mode

Citizens may opt to file complaints in Anonymous Mode. In this mode, public tracking, municipal officer dashboards, and transparency feeds will never display your citizen identity or contact information. Your details remain restricted exclusively to system audit protection against vandalism.

3. Mapping & Vision Classification

Our mapping interface uses OpenStreetMap tile servers. When using AI category suggestion, optional image classification transmits the uploaded photo to our secure vision AI provider solely for category inference, and images are never retained by third parties for model training.

4. Data Retention & Control

Resolved complaints and photographic resolution proof are retained for civic auditing purposes. You may request account deactivation or notification preferences update at any time via your citizen profile.

5. Contact Grievance Officer

For data privacy queries or statutory inquiries: privacy@civicpulse.gov.in
