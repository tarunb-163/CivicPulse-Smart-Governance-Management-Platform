Terms of Service & Citizen Charter

Effective as of October 2026 for all users of the CivicPulse Platform

1. Acceptable Use

CivicPulse is dedicated to municipal grievance filing, road safety reporting, sanitation tracking, and civic improvements. Citizens agree not to submit fraudulent reports, defamatory statements, or commercial spam. Submitting knowingly false reports may result in account lockout and IP blocking under municipal bylaws.

2. Duplicate Prevention & Community Support

To avoid clogging administrative queues, the system checks for existing reports within a 300-meter radius. Citizens agree that the municipal administration may merge identical grievances into a parent ticket while preserving your vote, priority score, and notification updates.

3. Service Level Agreements (SLA)

Resolution timeframes are calculated according to category severity (24 hours for water leaks/hazardous wires, 48 hours for streetlights and garbage, up to 72 hours for road resurfacing). In case of overdue complaints, the platform automatically escalates tickets to the Department Head and Municipal Administrator.

4. Reopen and Feedback Policy

Once an issue is marked RESOLVED by an officer with photo proof, citizens have exactly 7 days to inspect the work and reopen the complaint if unsatisfied. After 7 days without reopen, the complaint automatically transitions to CLOSED.

5. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the municipal corporation regulations and applicable state e-governance guidelines.
