How CivicPulse Works

A seamless 4-step pipeline from complaint registration to field verification

1. Report Issue

Upload photos, drop a map pin, and let our AI categorize your grievance instantly.

2. Smart Routing

Auto-routed to the exact municipal department and assigned to the least-loaded officer.

3. Timed Redressal

4. Verify & Rate

Review photographic resolution proof and grade municipal service quality.
