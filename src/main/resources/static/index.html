Citizen Redressal Workflow

Standard operating procedure from grievance filing to citizen confirmation

Step 1

Lodge Grievance

Fill form details, pinpoint GPS location on the map, and attach photo evidence.

Step 2

Automated Assignment

Portal issues unique acknowledgement receipt and routes case to jurisdictional officer.

Step 3

Field Inspection & Redressal

Officer inspects the site, executes remedial work, and uploads resolution photos within SLA.

Step 4

Citizen Verification & Feedback

Citizen verifies resolution, provides rating, or requests reopening if dissatisfied.
