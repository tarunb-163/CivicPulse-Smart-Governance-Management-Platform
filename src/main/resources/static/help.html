Help & Citizen User Guide

Learn how to lodge grievances, track their progress, and understand municipal SLA resolution standards.

How Grievance Redressal Works

Step 1

Lodge Grievance

Submit title, category, description, ward location, and photo evidence via web or mobile portal.

Step 2

Instant Acknowledgement

Receive unique reference code (e.g. CP-2026-XXXX) and printable official acknowledgement receipt with QR verification.

Step 3

Officer Assignment & Action

Auto-routed to the jurisdictional nodal department. Officer investigates and initiates remedial action within defined SLA.

Step 4

Resolution & Feedback

Citizen receives SMS/notification upon resolution. Verify before/after photos and submit satisfaction rating.
